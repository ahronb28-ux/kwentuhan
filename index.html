Connect Gemini

This version uses your own free Google Gemini API key, so the coach hears your actual voice and can comment on pronunciation. The key is stored only in this browser.

  1. Open aistudio.google.com/apikey and sign in with your personal Google account.
  2. Tap Create API key and copy it.
  3. Paste it below and tap Save and test.

Privacy: on Google's free tier, your requests, including voice clips, may be used to improve Google's products. Keep anything confidential, especially work details, out of practice.
